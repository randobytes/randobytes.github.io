Crash into pixels!

Galaxyz is a sandbox destruction-focused space shooter with pixel art graphics.

Blow up whatever you want with blasters and missiles. Customize and upgrade your battle rig. Join factions or lead your squad. Progress, grow stronger, and become a legend of the galaxy.

The game is fast-paced and easy to pick up and play, with a focus on the thrill of watching objects shatter into pixels.

  • Planned release: 2026
  • Platforms: PC, MacOS
  • Facebook group

Galaxyz key features

  • High-octane retro – enjoy large pixel art graphics boosted with light effects and animations!
  • Smash into pixels – blow up anything into a dazzling shower of pixels!
  • Build your ship – combine parts to assemble your personal battle rig!
  • Choose your side – join treasure hunters, galactic police, pirates… or crash them all!
  • Lead your squad – hire mercenaries and deploy drones to dominate the battlefield!
  • Bass at full volume – feel the roar of jet engines drifting to the phonk soundtrack!
  • Game never over – respawn in the same spot upon destruction and take revenge!
  • Become the legend – unlock upgrades, grow stronger, and be unstoppable!
