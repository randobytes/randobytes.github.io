Crash into pixels!

Galaxyz - is a top-down space shooter which combines pixel art graphics with modern effects and an addictive destruction mechanic.

You control a customizable spaceship with blasters and homing missiles navigating endless space, battling other ships, asteroids, explosive objects and dangerous bosses.

The game is fast-paced and designed to be easy to pick up and play, with a focus on the thrill of watching objects break apart pixel by pixel.

  • Planned release: Summer 2025
  • Platforms: PC, MacOS

Galaxyz key features

  • High octane retro - large pixel art graphics boosted with light effects and animations!
  • Smash into pixels - explode whatever you want into a shower of dazzling pixels!
  • Craft your ship - combine parts to assemble your personal battle rig!
  • Choose your side - join treasure hunters, galactic police, pirates... or crash 'em all!
  • Bass at full volume - feel the power of jet engines drifting to the phonk soundtrack!
  • Game never over - reintegrate in the same spot upon destruction to take revenge on the enemy!
